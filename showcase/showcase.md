# Fathom

A little light in a very deep ocean. Swim as a glowing forager through flooded stone passages, grazing plankton one mote at a time. Eat the last light in a maze and descend into a depth with more hunters and less reach for your sonar.

In the Kindle dive, your world is a circle in the dark. Eating makes that window grow, and the passages you discover stay remembered beneath the black water. A sonar pulse flows around corners, revealing routes and catching fleeting silhouettes. Beyond your window, even familiar ground disappears.

An amber glimmer might be a harmless drifter, or the Lanternjaw wearing its disguise. It hunts your brightness. The eyeless Gloamfin listens instead: your own sonar can call it to you, and its violet pings sweep the corridors. The Flarefish announces itself with a warm charge, then flashes through the rock. Its light can show you the way and expose you at the same moment.

Keep moving, take the corners, and save a cloud of ink for a hunter that sees. With each mouthful, the ocean gets a little brighter and the chase gets a little closer. How deep can your light carry you?
